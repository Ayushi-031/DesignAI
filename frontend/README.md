
# DesignAI – AI-Powered Smart Interior Design and Furniture Recommendation System

## 📌 Project Overview

DesignAI is an AI-powered interior design platform that helps users create personalized room designs and discover suitable furniture based on their room images and design preferences.

The system combines AI-based design generation with furniture recommendations and e-commerce functionality to provide an integrated interior design experience.

Users can upload a room image and specify their preferred interior style, color palette, budget, materials, room type, and other requirements. The AI Design Service processes these inputs to generate a customized interior design and recommend suitable furniture.

## 🧠 How DesignAI Works

1. User registers or logs into the system.
2. User uploads an image of their room.
3. User enters design preferences such as style, color, budget, material, and room type.
4. The AI Design Service processes the room image and user preferences.
5. A personalized interior design is generated.
6. Suitable furniture recommendations are provided.
7. Users can view product details and add selected furniture to their cart.
8. Users can place an order or save the generated project for future use.

## 🏗️ System Modules

### User Management

Handles user registration, authentication, login, logout, and profile information.

### Room Management

Stores uploaded room images and information such as room type, image URL, upload date, and description.

### AI Design Service

Processes room images and user preferences to generate personalized interior designs and furniture recommendations.

### Design Management

Stores and manages AI-generated designs, including design style, budget, generated image, and description.

### Furniture Management

Maintains furniture and product information such as name, category, price, description, material, dimensions, and images.

### Saved Projects

Allows users to save generated interior designs and access them later.

### Cart and Order Management

Provides functionality for adding furniture to the cart, placing orders, and managing order details.

### Admin Management

Allows administrators to manage users, products, categories, orders, and system reports.

## 🗂️ Database Entities

The major entities used in the system are:

* **USERS** – Stores user account and profile information.
* **ROOMS** – Stores uploaded room information and images.
* **DESIGNS** – Stores AI-generated design information.
* **SAVED_PROJECTS** – Stores projects saved by users.
* **FURNITURE** – Stores furniture and product information.
